Part 1 · Accountability

One name
on every Rock.

If everyone owns it, no one owns it. Accountability isn’t blame: it’s clarity, ownership and follow-through.

Part 1 · The tools

Rocks and To-Dos.

Rocks
90 days
  • A few per person, not a wish list
  • Measurable, with a clear “done when”
  • One owner, always
To-Dos
7 days
  • Short, specific promises
  • Done by next week’s L10
  • Or flagged early, never quietly late
