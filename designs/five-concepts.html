Who we are

A family in the oil business, here to serve yours.

Ashercrest Enterprises (A.C.E.) is a family-owned oil, gas, and lubricant company rooted in Pleasanton, Texas. We blend and sell our own oils, deliver on a schedule that fits your work, and offer straight, honest consulting to keep your equipment running. As a Yeshua-centered, God-first company, ahava (אהבה) — love — and integrity guide every load we send out. Our promise is simple: the customer comes first, every time.
